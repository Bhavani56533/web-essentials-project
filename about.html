Our History

The department started in November 2021 with 30 students and two laboratories. Today it supports 180 students every year and a team of 12 faculty members.

Mission and Objectives

  • Teach computing together with business subjects such as management, economics and finance, including simple ideas like profit etc.
  • Train students to build software solutions for real business problems.
  • Promote innovation, entrepreneurship and ethical business practice.

Facilities

Business Analytics Laboratory
40 workstations with tools for data analysis, dashboards and reporting.
Software Development Laboratory
Systems for web, database and enterprise application development.
Department Library
Over 1,000 books and journals on computing, management and finance, including case studies on reducing CO2 emissions in supply chains.
